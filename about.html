About CodeStart Academy

CodeStart Academy is a technology coaching centre built around one idea: students learn best by building things. We teach the skills that employers look for. Every class is live and online, so students can learn from any city.

Our courses cover Java, SQL, Spring Boot, Low Level Design, Data Structures and Algorithms, and HTML and CSS. Each course mixes concept sessions with practical exercises, so students finish with working projects, not just notes.

We keep our fees affordable so that talented students can learn without financial stress. Our classes are small, so every student gets attention.

Completely Online, Live Learning

Every CodeStart Academy class is fully online and live. You learn with a trainer in real time, ask questions as you go, and get your doubts cleared in the same session.

Our Class Schedule

  • Batch 1: Monday, Wednesday, and Friday
  • Batch 2: Tuesday, Thursday, and Saturday

Each batch meets three times a week, so you have time to practise between sessions.

Our Goal

Our goal is not to rush you through a course. It is to help you understand each topic comfortably, so you can build with confidence and carry the concepts forward to any technology.

We would rather you understand one concept well than rush through ten.

Our Mission

To turn curiosity into capability and potential into opportunity.

At CodeStart Academy, our mission is to make quality programming education accessible to every aspiring learner, regardless of their background or experience.

We focus on building strong programming fundamentals, developing logical thinking, and nurturing problem-solving skills that go beyond any single technology. Our goal is to empower students to learn with confidence, adapt to evolving technologies, and build a strong foundation for a successful career in the world of software development.

We don’t just prepare students for today’s technology. We prepare them for tomorrow’s possibilities.

What We Believe

  • Practice first: code every day, not only before exams.
  • Clear fundamentals: strong basics make every advanced topic easier.
  • Honest guidance: we tell students what to learn next and why.
